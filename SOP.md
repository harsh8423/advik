# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) & Project Proposal
## Advik Freight Website Enhancement

### 1. Project Overview
We propose a comprehensive enhancement of the Advik Freight digital presence, transforming the current website into a state-of-the-art, interactive platform. This project aims to leverage cutting-edge 3D web technologies and modern design principles to create an immersive user experience that effectively communicates Advik Freight's capabilities, industry expertise, and commitment to innovation. The goal is to build a robust, scalable, and visually stunning website that stands out in the logistics sector.

### 2. Demo Deliverables
We have successfully developed a functional demo of the Advik Freight website, showcasing the core design philosophy, 3D interactive elements, and key landing pages.
- **Existing Pages:**
    - **Home Page:** Featuring high-end 3D hero animations and interactive service previews.
    - **About Us:** Interactive storytelling of the company's mission and vision.
    - **Drayage:** Dedicated service page with specialized content.
    - **Warehousing:** Dedicated service page with 3D visualizations.

### 3. Scope of Work (Future Implementation)
The next phase of development focuses on expanding the website's content depth, enhancing user engagement through advanced 3D designs, and ensuring full responsiveness across all devices.

#### 3.1 New Service Pages
We will design and develop dedicated pages for the following services, maintaining the high visual standard set by the demo:
- **Air & Expedited:** Focusing on speed and global reach.
- **Intermodal:** Highlighting efficiency and multi-mode transport solutions.
- **Flatbed:** Showcasing capabilities for oversized and irregular cargo.
- **Reefer:** Emphasizing temperature-controlled logistics expertise.
- **LTL (Less Than Truckload):** detailed options for smaller shipments.
- **Cross Border:** Information on international logistics and customs compliance.

*Each service page will feature:*
- Custom 3D illustrations/animations relevant to the specific mode of transport.
- Detailed service descriptions and benefits.
- "Get a Quote" integration.

#### 3.2 Industry Vertical Pages
To demonstrate industry-specific expertise, we will create dedicated pages for:
- **Automotive:** Just-in-time delivery and parts logistics.
- **Food & Beverage:** Safety, compliance, and cold chain solutions.
- **Produce:** Freshness and time-sensitive transport.
- **Renewable Energy:** Logistics for large-scale energy components.
- **Retail:** Supply chain optimization for consumer goods.
- **Manufacturing:** Raw material and finished goods transport.

#### 3.3 Additional Functional Pages
- **Carrier Page:** A dedicated portal/page for carrier partners to register, view load boards, or access resources.
- **Blog/Insights Page:** A CMS-integrated blog section to share industry news, company updates, and thought leadership articles, improving SEO and user engagement.

#### 3.4 Technical & Design Requirements
- **Responsive Design:** Complete optimization for Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop devices. The 3D elements will be optimized to perform smoothly on mobile devices without compromising visual quality.
- **Advanced 3D Designs:** Continued implementation of interactive 3D elements (using WebGL/Three.js/React Three Fiber) to provide an immersive user experience that differentiates Advik Freight from competitors.
- **Dual Theme Support:** Implementation of a seamless Dark and Light mode toggle, allowing users to choose their preferred viewing experience while maintaining brand consistency in both themes.
- **Performance Optimization:** Ensuring fast load times despite rich media content.

### 4. Implementation Strategy
- **Phase 1: Content & Resource Collection:** Collaborating with the client to gather all necessary text, images, and brand assets.
- **Phase 2: Content Expansion:** Development of the new Service and Industry pages using the established design system.
- **Phase 3: Functional Pages:** Implementation of the Carrier and Blog sections.
- **Phase 4: Optimization:** Rigorous testing of responsive layouts, dual theme consistency, and 3D performance optimization across devices.
- **Phase 5: Final Review & Launch:** Client walkthrough, final adjustments, and deployment to production.

### 5. Approval & Next Steps
This document serves as the basis for the approval to proceed with the full-scale development. Upon approval, we will immediately commence with Phase 1.
